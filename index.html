FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is VPS hosting?

VPS hosting provides dedicated virtual resources on a shared physical server, giving you more control and flexibility than typical shared hosting.

Can I upgrade my VPS plan?

Yes. You can move to a larger plan when your website or application requires additional resources.

What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?

Annual billing charges the equivalent of ten monthly payments over a twelve-month period.

Do I get root access?

Yes. All four VPS plans include root access.
